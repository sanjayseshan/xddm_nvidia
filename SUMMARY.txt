Current checkpoint
------------------
v106 refines the accelerated display driver (nvxdisp.dll) based on physical
screen verification. In v105, the desktop initialized with accelerated fills,
but desktop icons rendered as cyan squares and mouse cursor/text were affected.
Root cause identified and addressed:
1. Masked blits (psoMask != NULL): Icons and cursors use quaternary ROPs with
   a mask bitmap. DrvBitBlt in v105 handled solid brush fills without checking
   psoMask, turning transparent icon/label masks into solid bounding-box fills.
   v106 strictly gates acceleration on (!psoMask), falling back to EngBitBlt
   so GDI applies icon transparency and text glyph masks properly.
2. Pointer Hooks: Added DrvSetPointerShape and DrvMovePointer to DrvFunctionTable
   delegating to EngSetPointerShape / EngMovePointer.
3. Graphics Capabilities: Set pdi->flGraphicsCaps = GCAPS_OPAQUERECT |
   GCAPS_MONO_DITHER (0x500) matching stock DEVINFO.
